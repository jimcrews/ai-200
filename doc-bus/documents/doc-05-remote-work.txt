Making Remote Work Effective

Many teams now work remotely for at least part of the week. Remote work removes the daily commute and gives people more control over their schedules, but it can also make collaboration and communication harder.

Successful remote teams tend to share a few habits. They write things down, so decisions and plans are easy to find later. They agree on core hours when everyone is available for meetings, and they use asynchronous tools such as shared documents and chat threads for everything else.

Managers play an important role as well. Regular one-on-one check-ins help people feel connected and catch problems early. Measuring results rather than hours online builds trust and lets team members organise their day in the way that suits them best.

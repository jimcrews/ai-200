Quarterly Sales Summary - Q3

Total revenue for the third quarter was $4.2 million, an increase of 8 percent compared with the previous quarter. Growth came mainly from the online store, which grew 15 percent, while sales in physical stores stayed flat.

The best-selling product line was outdoor furniture, helped by an early start to the warm season and a successful email campaign in August. Kitchenware sales fell slightly after a key supplier delayed two shipments.

For the fourth quarter, the team plans to expand the online catalogue, secure a second kitchenware supplier, and run a holiday promotion beginning in mid-November. The forecast for Q4 revenue is between $4.5 and $4.8 million.
